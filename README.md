# CommitX — MVP Design Prompt

Create a modern, premium, minimalist mobile application called **CommitX**.

Tagline:

**Commit. Complete. Get It Back.**

## App Vision

CommitX is an accountability app that helps people keep the promises they make to themselves.

Unlike ordinary timer apps, CommitX creates commitment.

The app should make users feel calm, focused, disciplined and motivated every time they open it.

The design should be inspired by the simplicity and elegance of Forest, but it must have its own unique identity.

Do NOT copy Forest's branding, illustrations, layouts or assets.

The entire UI should feel premium.

## Important

The Hourglass is the logo, loading animation and identity of CommitX.

Do NOT use circular progress rings.

Do NOT use trees.

Do NOT use coins.

Do NOT make it look like a Pomodoro app.

This is an accountability app.

The user is making a promise to themselves.

Every design decision should reinforce calmness, commitment and discipline.

The app should be understandable within 10 seconds of opening.

The overall experience should feel beautiful enough that users want to return every day.
